# Frontier closure and source-only reopening

## When this applies

Use after every admissible local strategy family has already completed its declared outcome-blind seed, independent identity Oracle, and one frozen strict-T+1 replay (or has failed an earlier support/semantic gate). It prevents “continuous research” from degrading into variants of a falsified object.

## Closure protocol

1. Reconcile every candidate family into one of: source unavailable, support failure, semantic failure, Oracle mismatch, frozen replay/economic failure, or promotion pass.
2. Freeze the usable definition **before** sourcing more data. A production-capable technical chain requires: same-source canonical universe, complete date-addressable history, outcome-blind support, independent Oracle equality, one frozen replay, fixed sample/year/month/T+1 gates, and a scanner that reproduces the decision-time contract.
3. Mark any closed ontology as non-reopenable by threshold, time-window, entry, stop, target, holding-period, year, symbol, or post-outcome bucket changes. Diagnostic bins may explain failure only.
4. Keep production `EMPTY_BOOK` and never route historical/research rows into candidates while no chain passes.

## Only legal next step: source-only qualification

A new provider or raw information dimension may reopen research only through a no-outcome source audit. Do not read prices, generate seeds, or design a strategy during this audit.

Predeclare and test:

- provider provenance and single-source price/volume series;
- canonical SH/SZ/BJ universe coverage;
- date-addressable history through every declared year;
- representative probes across exchanges and periods;
- full-session timestamp/slot completeness; and
- PIT/publication timestamps for non-price event sources.

A source failure closes that provider for the proposed ontology. Do not shorten the date range, fill missing intervals from another provider, or substitute cached subsets.

## Evidence example

A provider can honor an API request for a multi-year time range and a high row limit but still return only its retention window. Treat observed earliest timestamps—not request parameters—as the source-history evidence. This is a source-contract failure, not a strategy result.

## Handoff after a source pass

A passed source audit authorizes only a separate canonical-universe and slot-completeness audit. Only after that audit passes may a fresh, preregistered outcome-blind ontology begin its seed → independent Oracle → single frozen strict-T+1 replay chain.
