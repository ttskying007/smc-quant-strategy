# V431 Local Daily Pure-Structure Frontier Closure

## Trigger

Use when asked to continue SMC strategy research autonomously after multiple raw daily OHLCV signal generators have been tested, or when a historical high-WR strategy is being considered for current production.

## Predeclare the decision contract

For a new raw-daily structure ontology, do **not** open outcomes until it has:

1. Full universe coverage and at least 40 takeover seeds in each 2023–2026 year.
2. Independent raw-bar rederivation of event, POI, freshness, lifecycle (`touch -> reclaim -> hold`) and one-execution-per-symbol-day.
3. No outcome, exit, PnL, MFE/MAE, or future fields in seed inputs.
4. Fixed execution: first next-session open after takeover, with T+1=0.

A frozen 5D or 10D diagnostic is usable only if **every** year has `n>=40`, positive rate `>=50%`, average mark `>=0`, and zone invalidation `<=30%`. Aggregate averages, a strong single year, or a small high-WR pocket never substitute.

## Corrected semantic evidence

Older daily-combination evidence can be invalidated by lifecycle defects. Before relying on a replay, verify:

- An OB touched before its prerequisite event is not a fresh post-event retest.
- An FVG source/creation bar is not a post-creation retest.
- Lifecycle starts strictly after `max(event_idx, poi_idx)`.
- Each replay row maps back to exactly one ordered takeover seed.

V415–V419 applied these checks to strict R1/R2/C1 and verified 21,042 replay rows with zero chronology and T+1 failures. The corrected strict replays still produced zero annual gate passes.

## Historical high metrics are not current production evidence

A historical selection set may be research-valid yet production-invalid. Require all of:

- reproducible parent/raw rule in the current scanner;
- latest-date non-history actionable rows;
- independently rederived semantics and source lineage;
- full T+1 and surface-contract verification.

V246 is the reference pitfall: historical selected metrics were strong (`n=573`, WR 94.42%, average 7.60%), but the strict current parent covered only 3.6649% of historical selected rows and the 2026-07-13 current raw rule produced zero candidates. It must remain shadow-only; do not populate a current watchlist from historical trades.

## Stop rule and valid restart triggers

When R1–R5 are closed and no candidate has a distinct causal ontology on the same local daily OHLCV information set, stop strategy/replay/parameter iteration. Continue only operational monitoring and semantic/data/frontend consistency work.

V431 physically verifies this registry rather than relying on the narrative alone: `/root/.hermes/smc_audit/v431_local_daily_structure_frontier_closure_latest.json`, generated by `/root/.hermes/scripts/v25/v431_local_daily_structure_frontier_closure_audit.py`. The audit must show every R1/R2/C1/R3/R4/R5 branch as `CLOSED_ECONOMIC`, `audit_failures=[]`, no production/frontend/watchlist writes, and `unclosed_defined_local_daily_ontology_count=0` before declaring the local-daily frontier complete.

A restart is allowed only if:

1. A scanner produces a genuinely distinct, auditable event/POI ontology; or
2. A new information dimension passes full historical coverage, PIT/causality, and raw price-alignment admission before one frozen replay.

This prevents forcing an artificial R6 and turning disciplined research into endless iteration.
