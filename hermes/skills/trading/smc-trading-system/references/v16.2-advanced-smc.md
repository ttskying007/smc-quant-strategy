# V16.2 Advanced SMC Implementation Details

## Detection Functions

### detect_turtle_soup(daily, swings, atr) → list
Turtle Soup (Stop Hunt): price briefly breaks a swing high/low, triggers stops/chasers, then reverses within 1-3 bars.

Bullish: `bar['l'] < sw_price * 0.998` → within 3 bars: `daily[k]['c'] > sw_price * 1.01` + reversal >1%
Quality: A (>2% reversal) vs B (>1%)

Issue: A股日线0笔A级信号. Need wider window or looser reversal %.

### detect_inducement(daily, swings, ob_zones) → list
Inducement: price briefly dips below OB low (inducing shorts), then recovers and holds above.

Conditions:
- OB formed, j in ob_idx+2..ob_idx+15
- `bar['l'] < ob_low * 0.99` (dip <1%)
- Within 2-3 bars: `close > ob_low AND low > ob_low * 0.97` (recovery + no re-break)
- Recovery >1.5%

95% of V16.2 trades have IDM confirmation.

### detect_breaker_zones(signals, daily) → list
Bullish OB broken (`close < ob_low * 0.98`) → within 10 bars: `close > ob_low` → Breaker_Support

### detect_consolidation(daily, atr) → list
Looks for 15-25 bar consolidation (range < 2x ATR) followed by breakout. Most recent window where break exceeds range_high.

### auto_optimize_stock(daily, signals) → StockParams
4-dimension auto-optimization:

| Dimension | Condition | Adjustment |
|-----------|-----------|------------|
| ATR% > 5.0 | Extreme volatility | sl=2.0, tp=4.0/7.0, entry_buffer=0.03 |
| ATR% 3.0-5.0 | High vol | sl=1.5, tp=3.0/5.0 |
| ATR% 1.5-3.0 | Normal | sl=1.2, tp=2.5/4.5 |
| ATR% < 1.5 | Low vol | sl=0.8, tp=1.5/3.0, max_age=80 |
| OB density > 5% | Ranging stock | tp × 0.7, max_age=60 |
| OB density < 1% | Trending stock | tp × 1.3, max_age=150 |
| Price > 100 | High absolute | sl × 0.8 (tight) |
| Price < 10 | Low absolute | sl × 1.3 (wide) |
