# V20.9 Multi-Timeframe Resonance

## Weekly CHOCH/BOS Detection (`detect_weekly_choch`)

Detects structural breaks on weekly timeframe by scanning for price breaking above recent swing highs.

Multi-lookback approach: checks 5, 8, 12 bar windows for swing high breaks.

## Weekly Swing Structure (`detect_weekly_swings`)

Pivot detection with 2-bar left/right confirmation. Classifies into:
- HH: Higher High (price peak > all neighbors)
- LL: Lower Low (price trough < all neighbors)
- HL: Higher Low (swing low > previous swing low)
- LH: Lower High (swing high < previous swing high)

Trend determination: last two swing lows in recent 20 bars determine trend direction.

## Composite Score Grading Fix (V20.9)

**Problem**: V20.8 composite_score gave D-grade to trades without weekly data (96% of stocks have no weekly data). D-grade uses quality_sl_mult=1.5, quality_tp_mult=0.5, max_hold=12 — severely handicapping the trade.

**Fix**: When weekly/h60 data is unavailable, give neutral bonus (+2/+1) instead of zero. This prevents the grading system from punishing stocks that simply don't have MTF data.

**Impact**: avgPnL +8.88%→+13.19% (+49%). 140 D-grade trades upgraded to A/B/C grades with better quality-adjusted SL/TP parameters.
